# Drone parking

Open http://192.168.8.170:8765 and press **MAP**. Opening the map enables observation; it does not move or arm the car. Existing manual controls and Follow remain available. **PARK** requires a calibrated car pose and verified physical clearance. STOP, disconnect and sensor expiry brake parking; the operator can retain the normal armed session after STOP.

The observer accepts fresh, exact, untracked poses from drone tags 9, 12, 13 and 14. It maps another tag after three consistent co-visible observations. Other IDs, including incidental ID17, do not define the drone. IMU heading stabilizes rotation, with the calibrated camera/body mounting determining the heading axis. Monocular tag scale and camera intrinsics currently remain nominal: 50 mm black square and fx=fy=968 at1280×720. Displayed metre values are provisional.

The current map origin is the first drone tag. The blue point is the camera estimate until the camera-to-car and drone-to-tag transforms are calibrated. A tag is not assumed to be the drone's centre. No accelerometer translation, magnetic heading or motor-time-only position estimate is used. Ultrasonic/line sensor identity is unverified on this PCB, so those sensors have no obstacle authority.

Static room features are tracked between tag observations and triangulated after at least60 mm nominal translation. A temporary loss of tags can use a PnP pose from at least20 spatially distributed scene landmarks,60% inliers and low reprojection error. This bridge expires1.5s after the last tag anchor; failed or degenerate geometry never supplies a pose. It is an experimental bridge, not validated precision navigation. A final image without tags does not itself mean the car is parked.

Planning uses the complete car rectangle, inflated by clearance margin and localization uncertainty. Landing skids are capsules; inward leg slope further narrows the available corridor at car height. The search permits forward, reverse and in-place pivots, checking the swept rectangle at5 mm corner displacement increments with extra sampling margin. Turns are aligned outside the gap; each executed pulse also checks the predicted swept footprint using validated upper motion rates and stopping travel, rather than just the average response. The configured upper/stopping bounds are currently unverified. Repeated fresh poses within20 mm and3° of the route endpoint are required to finish. Search endpoints can be within20 mm of the requested centre, so this prototype does not promise zero-error parking.

Feedback uses full-strength Q/E-style pivots atPWM1800, bounded20–100 ms, and forward/reverse pulses bounded20–120 ms. The independent motor guard checks deadlines every10 ms. Each pulse brakes and settles before accepting a post-motion pose. IMU or localization expiry, ownership loss, STOP and disconnect stop execution. Camera motion and Follow cannot run concurrently with parking. A cancelled planning request cannot later start motion.

Configuration is `config/parking.json`; `WTS_PARKING_CONFIG` can select another file. Installed packages fall back to `default_parking.json`. Geometry, calibration, motion response and error bounds must be established from measurements before their confirmation flags are set. An approximate leg-spacing estimate must not be silently treated as an exact clear corridor. The configured vehicle width/length/height are the complete swept envelope, including wheels, camera and protrusions, not wheel track or wheelbase.

Transforms use OpenCV camera coordinates: x right, y down, z forward. `camera_to_car` maps camera coordinates into car coordinates; `drone_to_anchor` maps the configured anchor tag into drone coordinates. Both are rigid4×4 transforms. Car and drone planning frames use x right, y down, z forward, with yaw measured from +z toward +x. `parking_goal` specifies the desired car-centre pose in that drone frame, in metres and degrees. `camera_matrix`/`distortion` describe the1280×720 image; camera pan/tilt must match `camera_mount_servo`. Parking recomputes marker poses from detected corners with the configured intrinsics when their calibration flag is confirmed; ordinary Follow retains its existing nominal detector calibration.

Current physical estimates supplied by the operator: skid length≈304.8 mm and spacing≈254 mm. Actual clear width vs centre spacing, inward splay, overhead clearance, full car envelope and target centre remain unverified. Wheelbase210 mm and track149 mm do not establish the overall envelope.
